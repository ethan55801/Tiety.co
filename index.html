04 / Good questions

Before
we begin.

A few things that help you plan.

Can I combine more than one service?+

Yes. A website, custom AI tool, IT support, and network work can be scoped together. We can also begin with a focused task and agree later phases separately.

Can you improve an existing website or system?+

Start by sharing what you have and what is not working. A review helps identify what can be retained, what needs improving, and whether access or technical constraints affect the work.

How are cost and timelines decided?+

They depend on the scope, content, integrations, equipment, and access required. Priorities, deliverables, and milestones should be agreed before work begins.

What should I prepare for an AI project?+

A description of the workflow and a few non-sensitive examples are a useful start. We then identify permitted data, required integrations, review steps, and how to assess whether the output is useful.

What happens after the handover?+

Documentation and access arrangements are defined in the scope. Maintenance, additional development, and support can be discussed separately, including coverage and response expectations.
